iPhone Duo: Apple’s first foldable is here, from RM9,499

Apple finally made a foldable. The iPhone Duo went on sale on 23 October, and Malaysia was in the first wave. Getting one is the hard part.

What it is

  • A phone that opens like a book: a 5.4-inch outer screen, and a 7.6-inch screen inside.
  • Two 48MP cameras on the back, main and ultra wide. The 2x zoom is a crop of the main camera; there’s no separate telephoto lens.
  • Touch ID in the side button. No Face ID.
  • A20 Pro chip, IP68 water and dust rating, in star white or night sky.
  • Malaysia price: RM9,499 (256GB), RM10,499 (512GB), RM12,499 (1TB), RM15,499 (2TB). Pre-orders opened 16 October; it went on sale here on 23 October.

The forecast

  • IDC expects Apple to take 44% of foldable market value in 2026, on 31% of the units. Fewer phones, bigger slice of the money.
  • IDC puts Duo shipments at about 7 million this year, about 3% of all iPhones, growing another 70% next year.
  • Counterpoint is more careful: 6 million units or fewer in the near term.
  • The limit is supply, not demand. The hinge and the folding screen are hard to make, and only a few factories in China build them.

What reviewers are saying

The good: the hinge gets near-unanimous praise, and so do the inner screen and the speakers.

The not so good: people miss Face ID, the camera has no proper telephoto, it’s heavy, and a lot of apps aren’t ready for the new screen yet.

  • The crease: easy to miss head-on, but 9to5Mac says anyone calling it invisible “is misleading you.”
  • Battery: split. Wired ended the day with about 40% left; The Verge says it doesn’t last a full day of heavy use.
  • The verdict: The Verge calls it “incomplete as a product, but complete enough as an idea.” Marques Brownlee says that for someone who mostly texts and browses, it’s “probably a no.”
